About Me

I am pursuing an M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence Systems at NUS-ISS (Aug 2025 – present), after a B.Eng. in Artificial Intelligence at Anhui University (2021 – 2025). My work spans robot learning, 3D medical image analysis, computer vision, and clinical machine learning.

I am interested in AI systems that learn from complex real-world data, whether robot interaction or volumetric medical scans, and that stay reliable and interpretable enough for people to trust and review.
